# Journal

### Monday, March 3, 2025, 11:47pm

Bombed the systems design interview at Stripe. Halfway through I forgot what consistent hashing was — like the actual definition just left my head — and the interviewer's face changed and after that nothing I said landed. I know I'm not getting this one.

The worse thing is that I had been kind of telling myself that this was going to be the job that fixed everything. New city, more money, finally working with people who are actually good. Now I'll be stuck where I am forever. Nobody good is going to want to hire me after a screwup like this. Mia keeps saying "it's one interview" but she doesn't get it — at this level you don't get a second chance, the recruiters all talk to each other. I should have just stayed at my current job and not tried.

### Tuesday, March 4, 2025

Couldn't sleep. Kept replaying the moment I went blank.

Talked to Ralph at lunch and he said something that stuck — "you bombed one round, not your career." I don't entirely buy it but it loosened something. I went for a long run after work, listened to the Tycho album the whole way through, and by the end I felt better. Stopped at the bookstore on 7th and bought *Meditations* on a whim. Thought about Mia, and about how lucky I am to have someone who lets me be miserable about something stupid for two days without making it a problem.

### Wednesday, March 5, 2025

Recruiter from Stripe emailed. They want to do another round. Apparently the systems design interviewer thought I "recovered well" and wanted to see more. So much for the recruiters all talking to each other.

I feel kind of stupid about Monday's entry. I always do this — convince myself the worst possible reading of an event is the only correct one, then act surprised when reality is more boring. Saving this thought somewhere I'll see it.

### Saturday March 8

Mia and I drove up to Ojai. The Airbnb was a small adobe house at the end of a dirt road, no neighbours, just hills. We made pasta and drank a bottle of red and sat on the porch listening to the coyotes. She asked me if I was "actually okay" about Monday and I said yes, and then realised partway through saying it that it was actually true.

I'm not going to pretend a weekend in Ojai fixes anything. But the way I feel right now — with my legs on her lap and the dog asleep on the rug and the dishes still on the table — I can't access the version of me that wrote Monday's entry. I genuinely cannot remember what that felt like.

### Sunday, March 9, 2025, 9:12pm

Drove back this afternoon. Got coffee at that place in Santa Paula on the way down. Mia drove the second half so I could nap.

The week ahead is going to suck — three deliverables, the second Stripe round on Friday, my mom is in town starting Wednesday. But it feels manageable in a way that it didn't last Monday. I don't know if that's because the actual workload changed or because I'm in a better place. Probably both.

### Wednesday March 12

Mom got in last night. She immediately asked if I'd called Aunt Sofia (I hadn't), if I was eating enough (I am), and whether Mia was "the one" (jesus christ, mom). I love her but two hours in and I'm already exhausted.

I notice I get small with her. I revert to being seventeen — defensive, sullen, monosyllabic. I hate that I do this and I know it makes her sad. Tomorrow I'm going to try to actually be present, to ask her about her week and not act like every question is an attack. Setting the intention here so I can't pretend I didn't.

### Friday, March 14, 2025, 6:30pm

Stripe round 2 went well. I think. The pair-programming part flowed and I caught myself enjoying the algorithms round, which is new. The behavioural was fine — I told the story about migrating the analytics pipeline at my current job and it landed. We'll see.

Mom and I had dinner at the Vietnamese place she liked last time. I asked her about her book club and her garden and the dog, and she opened up in a way she usually doesn't. At one point she said "you seem different — calmer," and I almost cried at the table. I held it together. But I'm writing it down so I remember she said that.

### Saturday March 15

Stripe offer came in this morning. Senior level, 30% bump. They want an answer by Wednesday.

I don't know what I expected to feel and it's not this. I thought I'd be elated and instead I feel kind of hollow, like the version of me that needed this two weeks ago isn't around to enjoy it. I should be talking to Mia about whether we're moving. I should be calling my manager. Instead I'm sitting at the kitchen table writing in this thing.

Maybe that's the work — to actually let good news be good news, instead of immediately turning it into the next problem to solve. Going to close the laptop and go find Mia.
